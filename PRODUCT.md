# Dictation Notes website

## Register

brand

## Users

Meeting participants, interviewers, students and people handling customer conversations who want live transcripts and useful reply cues. Visitors need the correct Mac or iOS download, clear setup help, and an accurate explanation of data handling.

## Product Purpose

Introduce the released native Mac, iPhone and iPad apps, show their real interfaces, explain free dictation and the optional one-time cloud/AI unlock, and provide support and privacy information in all 13 supported languages.

## Brand Personality

Calm, practical, candid. A bright cool-white website with blue accents aligned with the native app and its waveform icon. The user's 2026-10-13 instruction replaces the earlier dark website palette across every page and language.

## Anti-references

Generic AI subscription landing pages, invented testimonials or ratings, fabricated product screenshots, and unconditional offline or zero-retention promises.

## Design Principles

- Real application screenshots explain the workflow.
- Platform requirements and differences appear beside the download choices.
- Free dictation, app unlock and provider charges are explained separately.
- Product facts, support, privacy and assistant-readable material agree.
- Every language has readable static HTML and crawlable links.
- Keep all pages light, with generous whitespace, clear sans-serif hierarchy and quiet blue surfaces.
- Functional documentation uses the same navigation, color tokens and spacing as the product page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable body text, WCAG AA text contrast, visible keyboard focus, 44px navigation targets, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts at 375/768/1440px, and Arabic right-to-left layout. Content and language navigation work without JavaScript.

## Source of context

Existing README, i18n copy and CSS; Mac 1.2.2 and iOS 1.1.1 approved metadata, the public App Store pages verified on 2026-11-14 and the shipped source. Apple Watch recording uses a paired iPhone. This is an update to the established website.
